Move `SolverVBD` rigid contact points with first-order body motion within each step and damp compliant rigid contacts on the penetration increment instead of the approach velocity. Evaluate strongly damped transitions relative to compression onset to preserve small force increments. Trajectories of rotating and damped contacts (including the default shape `kd`) change. Pass `rigid_contact_damping_bidirectional=True` for signed velocity damping in both directions, or re-tune `kd`. The solve metric of sliding rigid-rigid friction is now the secant of the Coulomb projection, continuous at the cone boundary; `friction_epsilon` no longer affects compliant-ALM rigid-rigid contacts.
