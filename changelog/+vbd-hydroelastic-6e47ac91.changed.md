Use positive `Contacts.rigid_contact_stiffness` and `rigid_contact_damping` in place of the shape-pair `ke` and `kd` in `SolverVBD` rigid contacts, and scale the mixed sliding `mu` by a positive `rigid_contact_friction`, so hydroelastic contacts use their `kh`-derived stiffness instead of the shape `ke`. Without a per-contact damping, a per-contact stiffness below the shape-pair `ke` scales `kd` by the same ratio. Re-tune `kh` and `kd` of VBD scenes with hydroelastic shapes.
