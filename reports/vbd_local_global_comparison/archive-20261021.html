Accuracy and remaining limits

Useful structural correction, not a universal improvement. The previously selected G1 budgets were retested. Stiff droop still fails the 0.1 mm shape target. Pile is a reference-only control. Lower gaps or quieter videos do not establish force balance, dynamic accuracy or equal-accuracy speedup.

Current G1 pile late overlap 0.1436 mm, peak ground depth 1.691 mm. Contact force consistency and difficult stiff cases remain unresolved. The separate CPU reversing-pull test misses its displacement bound; this review does not certify every supported mechanism.
