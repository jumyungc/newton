Accuracy and remaining limits

Useful lower budgets on bounded tests, not universal convergence. Ordinary droop, DR Legs, Slinky, cross-slide and Y-junction passed their stated limited checks in three repeats. Stiff droop still has 0.411 mm final shape error and fails the 0.1 mm target. Fourfold timestep refinement worsened the tested error to 2.7–2.8 mm. Pulley I1 lost contact and was rejected; the displayed pulley retains I5.

The retained pile has 0.1481 mm late overlap and 1.635 mm peak ground penetration; neither method is penetration-free. Slinky/contact trajectories are not bitwise repeatable. More iterations did not improve every check. Lower residual or quieter motion is not a physical-accuracy or passivity proof.

What still needs fixing

Not universally fastest, convergent or ready to claim a clean final solution. Stiff-droop accuracy and raw force balance remain unresolved; pulley I1 escapes. Historical Toy3/Toy4 results are preserved in the previous report and are not rerun or relabeled as current-source certification here. The separately requested full-gravity Toy4 BA µ=0.25/0.20 tuning and exact external-implementation comparison remain separate unfinished work.

The ordinary-droop I2, Slinky I4, DR I1, cross-slide I2 and Y I1 choices pass explicit limited screens, not universal accuracy certificates. Slinky I3 was withdrawn after extended repeats and a separately audited event-window correction; its initial successes and subsequent failures remain preserved. Most screens were chosen after an exploratory sweep; strict droop targets preceded it. Pile and pulley keep I5; stiff droop remains a failed I4 reference. Timestep and mesh convergence, force/friction consistency and difficult native-order cases remain open.
