What still needs fixing

Not regression-free or universally fastest. The droop accuracy regression persists. Ordinary Toy3/Toy4 pass the authored motion screens in both directions, but the stiff Toy4 variant still fails: AB reaches −91.656° and 1.518 mm slider overshoot, and BA misses its first extension phase. These results are not hidden by the rendered successes.

At equal I5 budgets, G1 costs roughly 1.68–2.32× the local route in the five cable fixtures. It is valuable where its additional convergence allows fewer local iterations; it is not automatically a win everywhere. Higher late motion in slinky/pulley remains an accuracy question, not evidence of a correct or incorrect energy balance by itself.

Except where stated otherwise, cable timing is the full-trajectory mean GPU frame time including collision, excluding the first three frames, compilation, rendering and host diagnostics. Rod-anchor RMS spans all recorded frames and rod joints. No historical runtime is mixed into these current tables. The report shows the retained solver, not an unqualified experimental improvement.
