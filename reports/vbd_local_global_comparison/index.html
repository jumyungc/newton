A controlled comparison of upstream local VBD and the same VBD solver with one experimental early global structural correction (G1).

Reading the report. Smaller named errors are better; motion, contact, settling, and cost must be read together. Focused step times use the median of three no-render CUDA-graph trajectories where available. The two routes use identical coefficients unless a section explicitly labels a bounded tuned comparison.

Cable droop: motionless does not mean numerically converged

“Settled” means that visible motion and recent swing are small. “Numerically converged” means that the constraint/force residual is small and the cable agrees with the independently solved equilibrium. Damping can produce the first without the second when a local iteration budget cannot propagate a stiff response through the full cable.

Exact supplied geometry and mass conventions, no contacts, bend K=0.1, stretch=shear K=1e4/1e6. The independent nonlinear discrete-rod equilibrium predicts 318.426/318.330 mm droop. Both dynamic runs use 6000 frames and ten captured substeps/frame.
