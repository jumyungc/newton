An experimental opt-in global structural correction for local VBD. The refreshed headline comparison uses latest upstream main, latest scalar PCR, latest sparse-direct VBD, and our current Local–Global VBD branch.

Reading the report. Smaller named errors are better; motion, contact, settling, and cost must be read together. Work differs by method: Local–Global VBD uses one early 6D correction, PCR corrects eligible translation paths every iteration, and sparse direct couples the articulation every iteration. Focused step times use the median of three no-render trajectories where available. Coefficients are method-tuned only where explicitly stated. Exact repository revisions and dirty-state metadata are recorded in the machine-readable results.
