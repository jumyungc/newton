Accuracy and remaining limits

Useful structural correction, not a universal improvement. In the first repeated Y-junction pair, G1 reduces trajectory RMS rod gap from 46.556 to 0.072 µm at nearly unchanged full-step cost. DR Legs loop-closure RMS falls from 2.609 to 0.076 mm, but G1 costs more. Stiff droop still fails the 0.1 mm shape target. Pile is a reference-only control. Lower gaps or quieter videos do not establish force balance, dynamic accuracy or equal-accuracy speedup.

Current G1 pile late overlap 0.1473 mm, peak ground depth 0.828 mm. Contact force consistency and difficult stiff cases remain unresolved. The previous CPU reversing-pull regression now passes the focused controls. A separate rotating-prismatic internal-torque defect remains under review; this page does not certify every supported mechanism.
