Impact comparisons

Slow-to-fast cable-curtain impact

A prescribed sphere moves slowly through the first cable curtain, crosses the remaining curtains at 160 m/s, and then stops while the cables ring down. The fast 1/60-second interval is shown 360× slower.

Small projectile through ten compact cable layers

A 45 mm-radius prescribed sphere crosses ten tightly spaced, staggered cable layers at 38 m/s. The layers form a three-dimensional lattice along and across the flight direction. All ten layers record projectile contact during impact and coupled ring-down.

Kinematically twisted cable bundle

Seven suspended cables were clamped at both ends and driven through four full relative turns. The captured fixed six-step control exceeded the combined contact and joint limits in 91 of 240 frames. The adaptive result completed all four turns with zero debt.

Scope of these results

These measurements demonstrate the tested scenes and operating ranges; they are not a proof for every shape, speed, or collision path. The high-drop p95 exceeds a 60 Hz frame budget, and emergency fine-resolution cable cases can be much more expensive than their median behavior suggests.

All simulations and Newton ViewerGL captures shown here ran on an NVIDIA L40. Implementation and research details are retained in the local project record.
