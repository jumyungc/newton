Scope

These are measured outcomes for the listed scenes, shapes, speeds, tolerances, and step ladder—not a proof for every possible collision path. The VBD simulation, fresh-contact validation, and rendering ran on Horde using the private research checkout and unified compliant ALM. The AVI / ACM section is a separate research prototype; its contact evidence is limited to the independent-contact case stated above.
